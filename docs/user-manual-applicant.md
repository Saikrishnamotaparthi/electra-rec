# Applicant User Manual

Welcome to the G-ELECTRA Recruitment Platform. This guide walks you through applying to join the club.

## Getting Started

1. Open your browser and go to the recruitment page: `/apply`
2. You can also reach it from the landing page by clicking **Apply Now** or **Join Team G-ELECTRA**

## Step-by-Step Application

### Step 1: Personal Details

Fill in the required personal information:

- **Full Name** — as it appears on your college records
- **Registration Number** — your GITAM registration number (e.g., `21B81A05XX`)
- **Phone Number** — 10-digit Indian mobile number (with or without +91)
- **Email Address** — your GITAM email (recommended) or any valid email
- **Academic Year** — select 1st, 2nd, 3rd, 4th, or Other
- **Branch** — select your department (CSE, ECE, EECE, Mechanical, etc.)

> 💡 Your progress is saved automatically as you type. You can leave and return later.

### Step 2: Portfolio / Team

Choose the **one** team that best matches your interests:

| Team | Focus |
|------|-------|
| Marketing Team | Campaigns, social media, brand outreach |
| Content Team | Writing, documentation, event reporting |
| Creative Design Team | Posters, banners, branding, reels |
| Web Developer | Websites, UI/UX, SEO |
| Prototype Makers – Hardware | IoT, robotics, hardware systems |
| Prototype Makers – Software | Apps, prototypes, integrations |

### Step 3: Role

Select the role you're applying for:

- **Member** — join a team and contribute
- **Co-Lead** — take on a leadership role in a portfolio

### Step 4: Questions

The questions depend on your role and membership status:

**All applicants answer:**
- Why do you want to join G-ELECTRA?
- What do you hope to learn or achieve?

**Co-Lead applicants also answer:**
- What motivates you to lead?
- (If existing member) Which team are you currently in?
- (If not a current member) Leadership experience, conflict handling, initiative examples, task prioritization

**Member applicants also answer:**
- Any relevant team experience
- Your commitment level (hours/week)

### Step 5: Photo

Provide a **Google Drive share link** to your photo:

1. Upload your photo to Google Drive
2. Right-click → **Share** → **Anyone with the link** → **Viewer**
3. Copy the link and paste it into the form

Accepted link formats:
- `https://drive.google.com/...`
- `https://docs.google.com/...`
- `https://drive.usercontent.google.com/...`

### Step 6: Review & Submit

- Review all your answers carefully
- Edit any step using the step navigation
- Click **Submit Application**

## After Submitting

1. You'll see a confirmation screen with your **Application ID** (format: `GE26-XXXXX`)
2. A confirmation email will be sent to your registered email address
3. Save your Application ID for future reference

## What Happens Next?

1. The G-ELECTRA team reviews all applications
2. Shortlisted candidates are contacted via email
3. Interaction/selection rounds follow
4. Final team assignments are communicated by email

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | Solution |
|---------|----------|
| Form won't let me proceed | Check that all required fields are filled (marked with *) |
| Email not received | Check spam/junk folder; ensure the email address is correct |
| Can't submit photo | Ensure the Google Drive link is shared publicly |
| Draft lost | Ensure you used the same browser/device; drafts are stored locally |
| Registration number error | Enter at least 3 characters; uppercase is auto-applied |

## Need Help?

Contact the G-ELECTRA recruitment team through the official club channels or email listed on the landing page.
